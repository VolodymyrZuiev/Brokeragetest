Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

What is Brokerage CRM?

Brokerage CRM is an all-in-one logistics platform designed to help freight brokers, carriers, and 3PLs manage loads, track fleets, automate compliance, and handle invoicing in a single unified dashboard.

How long does implementation usually take?

Thanks to our intuitive design and dedicated onboarding team, most companies are fully transitioned and operational within 7 to 14 days, including data migration from legacy systems.

Do you integrate with existing load boards?

Yes. We offer seamless API integrations with major industry load boards like DAT, Truckstop, and 123Loadboard, allowing you to post and search for capacity without leaving the platform.

Is there a mobile app for drivers?

Absolutely. We provide a native mobile app for iOS and Android that allows drivers to submit BOLs, provide live GPS tracking updates, and automate check-calls directly from their phones.

How secure is our company and financial data?

Security is our top priority. We use bank-level AES-256 encryption, regular third-party security audits, and strict role-based access controls (RBAC) to ensure your data is always protected.

Can I customize the reporting dashboards?

Yes, our analytics engine is highly customizable. You can drag and drop widgets, create custom KPI metrics, and set up automated email reports for different team members or clients.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include 24/7 live chat and email support. Enterprise clients receive a dedicated account manager, priority phone support, and custom onboarding sessions for their entire team.

How is the pricing structured?

We offer a simple, transparent per-user subscription model billed either monthly or annually. There are no hidden fees, and you only pay for the active seats your team uses.
